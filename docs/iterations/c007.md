stress_probe.py runs two warmup cycles and16 measured cycles with16 slots.
It cancels IDs1/5/9/13 after exactly two frames and reuses those same IDs with
new seeds in the next tick. Each measured cycle requires16 complete outputs,
four CANCELLED prefixes and empty final state:256 complete and64 cancelled per
arm. Prefixes are real .pcm artifacts and never counted as complete generations.
assess_stress.py reopens WAV/prefix bytes and checks frames, hashes, chunks,
unique generations, coverage and exact paired complete outputs.

After warmup, KV/graph structure must remain stable; allocated-memory range must
be <=64MiB. Structural growth is INCONCLUSIVE; excess allocated growth with fixed
structure is FAIL; reserved memory is reported. Missing evidence never passes.
This is deterministic direct-core stress, not HTTP disconnect validation or a
production-duration soak. No new speed claim is made by the stress checks.
